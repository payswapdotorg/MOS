# MOS Product Console — v1.6 Growth Autonomy Contract

Status: Product implementation contract
Architecture authority: v1.6 frozen documents

## 1. Primary experience

The user's primary question becomes: What outcome are you trying to achieve, and should MOS find the route for you?

Entry choices:
- Grow a creator channel
- Market a product
- Find a product to sell
- Generate leads
- Generate revenue

The mission form accepts target, constraints, connected accounts, optional product URL, optional authorized source repository, optional store and autonomy preference.

## 2. Creator growth

The mission view shows target, current progress, active hypothesis, current strategy, connected platforms, content pipeline, rights status, experiment state, platform health and next planned action.

Example:
Crypto channel
Target: 1,000,000 views
Progress: 184,320
Current hypothesis: market-event explainers with original commentary are outperforming evergreen tutorials.
Connected: YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X
Next: two experiments queued
Attention: one source requires rights review; one platform shows a distribution anomaly.

## 3. Product marketing

The product mission begins from a product/site/repository.

The UI displays product understanding, audience hypotheses, problem/value hypotheses, recommended platform mix, recommended success metrics, current experiments, traffic → conversion path and revenue/qualified-signup outcomes.

The user may override recommendations before autonomy starts.

## 4. Commerce discovery

The mission can begin with: I don't know what to sell.

MOS returns a research workspace showing evidence, not fabricated certainty:
- market candidates;
- product candidates;
- observed demand;
- content response;
- estimated economics;
- operational risks;
- test plan.

Once a candidate crosses the mission's viability gate:
candidate validated → list product → generate content → distribute → measure store visits → observe orders → update economics/learning.

## 5. Multi-platform control

The mission can connect any subset of supported platforms.

The user can choose one platform, a fixed platform portfolio, or choose for me.

Choose for me means MOS may change the platform mix based on evidence, capability, quota and platform-health conditions.

## 6. Content candidate and rights review

Every candidate should reveal source, source platform, observed evidence, rights state, transformation plan, expected value drivers, target platforms and publication status.

No grey/unverified rights state appears as an auto-approved action.

## 7. Platform health

Use descriptive language:
- healthy;
- degraded;
- distribution anomaly suspected;
- publishing restricted;
- authorization expired;
- quota constrained;
- automation/inauthenticity risk suspected;
- human review required.

Never present an inferred shadow ban as a confirmed platform fact.

## 8. Scientific trace

Question → Research → Evidence → Hypothesis → Experiment → Publication → Measurement → Analysis → Decision → Learning → Next experiment.

Observed, inferred and causal states remain visually distinct.

## 9. Commerce trace

Market → Product → Content → Distribution → Visit → Product interaction → Order → Margin → Learning.

Attribution is shown separately from causal inference.

## 10. Human-required action

Example: MOS paused this mission because a social platform requires additional authorization.
Example: MOS paused this candidate because the source licence could not be established.

The user gets a direct action and a resume path; the system does not hide the blocker.

## 11. Product constraints

The console must never promise that a target will be reached, imply an inferred restriction is a confirmed shadow ban, treat a viral source's performance as causal proof, show a rights-cleared badge without persisted evidence, bypass server authorization, or expose provider credentials.

## 12. Human growth

When automated distribution is not sufficient, the mission can add a human experiment arm.

Examples:
- UGC creator offer;
- creator post offer;
- creator-ad/authorization offer;
- product trial/review offer;
- human review or approval.

The mission view shows:
- brief and required deliverables;
- eligible creator/human capabilities;
- compensation terms;
- disclosure requirements;
- usage/redistribution rights;
- target platforms;
- expected and measured outcomes;
- experiment treatment identity.

Human work is authentic creator activity, not purchased engagement. MOS never commissions fake likes, comments, testimonials or other inauthentic signals.

Human-created assets enter the same rights/provenance and transformation pipeline as other content.
