# MOS Operating Graph and Decision Ledger — v1.5

## Operating Graph

Derived graph over canonical MOS records:

Prospect → Client → Goal → Strategy/Hypothesis → Playbook Version → Deployment → Workflow → Task → Human/AI/App/Extension → Execution → Evidence → Outcome → Revenue/Cost/Margin → Decision → Learning.

The graph gives employees and AI one operational context while preserving existing module authorities.

Rules: canonical source IDs are retained; derived edges may be rebuilt; client/workspace isolation is checked before traversal; historical versions remain addressable; unknown, observed, predicted, attributed and causal states stay distinct; read models cannot mutate authority directly.

## Decision Ledger

Material recommendations and commercial decisions can record decision scope, objective, evidence, context, hypothesis summary, expected impact and uncertainty, expected cost, proposer, alternatives, disposition, execution/deployment reference, observed outcome, learning reference and audit metadata.

The ledger is append-oriented. Corrections create new records rather than rewriting history.

## Profit Intelligence

Derived analytics over authoritative records can calculate revenue, delivery cost, human capacity cost, AI/provider cost, utilization, scope leakage, estimated/realized margin and client/service/project contribution. Material figures include source references, calculation version and assumptions.

Profit Intelligence recommends but does not mutate contracts, billing or financial-ledger authority.

## AI Operator

The Command Center may rank attention items such as blocked work, approvals, client risk, anomalies, scope leakage, margin pressure, capacity constraints and opportunities. Consequential actions continue through existing policy and approval rules.

## Client Operating Memory

Client memory is a governed projection over canonical client, goal, playbook, deployment, evidence, experiment, outcome, decision and learning records. Retrieval indexes may be used, but PostgreSQL remains authoritative.

## Sales-to-delivery continuity

Commercial proposals may carry structured scope, goals, outcomes and economics into the initial Playbook/Deployment path without manual re-entry. Provenance and version identity are preserved.
